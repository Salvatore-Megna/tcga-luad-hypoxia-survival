\noindent{\bfseries Research question}\par
Are gene-expression-derived hypoxia scores associated with overall survival in lung adenocarcinoma after adjustment for clinical characteristics?
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Data and methods}\par
TCGA-LUAD PanCancer Atlas clinical records and Buffa, Ragnum and Winter hypoxia scores. Cohort flow: 566 initial patients, 485 complete analysis cases, including 177 deaths.

Kaplan--Meier estimation, log-rank comparisons, multivariable Cox models, proportional-hazards diagnostics and sex-by-stage stratification.
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Selected results}\par
\begin{itemize}
\item Overall survival estimates at 12, 36 and 60 months: 0.875, 0.615 and 0.392.
\item In the reported Cox model, a 10-unit Buffa-score increase has HR 1.117 (95\% CI 1.036--1.206).
\item Historical global Schoenfeld tests give $p=0.016$ before and $p=0.461$ after stratification.
\end{itemize}
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Interpretation and limitations}\par
These are observational associations, not clinical decision rules. Hypoxia signatures are indirect measures, subgroup sizes are limited, and some detailed diagnostic plots still need historical cross-checks.
